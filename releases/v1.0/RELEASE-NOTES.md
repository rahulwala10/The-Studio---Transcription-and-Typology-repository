**The Studio Captions v1.0** — dynamic captions for Reels, by Rahul Wala.

Open it: https://rahulwala10.github.io/The-Studio---Transcription-and-Typology-repository/

**What's in this version**
- 27 mixed-font templates under "The Studio", each with colour presets
- Stand-out words picked automatically after transcription (punch word, names, numbers), and a focus word on every other caption, so the font pairing always shows
- Captions cut where a reader expects: at sentence ends, commas and pauses, never straight after "a", "the" or "to"
- Words light up with the voice (a small timing lead on in-browser transcriptions)
- One caption style per Reel (no extra sheets)
- Export defaults to Very High on a laptop, High on a phone

**Downloads**
- `the-studio-captions-v1.0-windows.zip` — the laptop copy: unzip, then double-click `Start The Studio Captions.bat`
- `source/the-studio-captions-source.zip` (repository root) — the full source code
