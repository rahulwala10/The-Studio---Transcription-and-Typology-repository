**The Studio Captions v1.1**: dynamic captions for Reels, by Rahul Wala.

Open it: https://rahulwala10.github.io/The-Studio---Transcription-and-Typology-repository/
How it works: https://rahulwala10.github.io/The-Studio---Transcription-and-Typology-repository/how-it-works.html

**What's new**
- 20 new templates in three new groups:
  - Cinematic: Bath, Piccadilly, Windsor, Ealing (typed letter by letter), Greenwich, Elstree
  - Professional: Nariman, Fleet, Cambridge, Parel, Churchgate, Shoreditch, Canary
  - Creative: Kala Ghoda, Hackney, Peckham, Manchester, Hampstead, Byculla, Brixton
- Each template has a set number of words per caption, chosen for its pace (1-3 for trailer cards, 3-6 for talking, 5-8 for calm subtitles)
- Better colours on the first 27: every colour pair now meets 7:1 contrast on video (4.5:1 on plates), checked by script; thin font weights replaced with Regular
- Edge contrast control on every template: a soft dark edge that keeps text readable on bright footage
- Reels safe-zone guide in the editor (phone button in the top bar)
- New home page with a live template reel, and a How it works page that also opens offline on the laptop
- 13 new fonts: Newsreader, Bodoni Moda, Jost, Libre Franklin, Instrument Sans, Archivo, Lexend, Space Grotesk, Figtree, Syne, Courier Prime, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Special Elite

**Downloads**
