**The Studio Captions v1.2**: dynamic captions for Reels, by Rahul Wala.

Open it: https://rahulwala10.github.io/The-Studio---Transcription-and-Typology-repository/
How it works: https://rahulwala10.github.io/The-Studio---Transcription-and-Typology-repository/how-it-works.html

**What's new**
- **Apply to all.** Every style tab (Typography, Style, Position, Motion, Effects, Layout) says it changes every caption, and shows which captions or words have their own changes, with one tap to make them follow. Change one caption or word in the video, then press Apply to all (all captions, all stand-out words, all regular words, or every word).
- **Save as my template.** Keeps colours, fonts, sizes, position, layout and motion as a template of your own, and can make it the starting template for every new project.
- **Hindi and Hinglish to English captions.** Pick "Hindi or Hinglish" and keep "Write the captions in English" ticked. Whisper translates the speech to English, timed to your voice. Small (or Medium) translates best.
- **Best export quality.** New Best setting (about 30 Mbps at 1080p), now the default on laptop and phone. A video smaller than 1080 x 1920 is exported at Instagram size with sharp captions, and the dialog says why the source was small. Resized frames are now smoothed properly (they were nearest-neighbour before).
- Home page and How it works page updated.
